# Sound credits

Every sound here is public domain, under **CC0 1.0** (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Each
licence was checked on the pack's own page. The clips were trimmed, made mono, levelled and converted to mp3 for
the game; the ambience loops have their ends crossfaded so they repeat without a seam.
